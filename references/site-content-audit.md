# Site inventory, audit, and ongoing governance
Source: Paula Ladenburg Land, *Content Audits and Inventories*, 2nd ed. (2023), chapters 4, 7–8, 11, 13–16, 19–20, 23–26; appendices A, C, E, H.

## Inventory != audit
Inventory describes what exists; audit evaluates usefulness and fitness for purpose. Begin with crawl/sitemap/CMS/export and note coverage gaps. Never extrapolate sampled pages into a complete inventory.
Inventory fields: url, canonical, page_type, title, locale, content_owner, publication_date, last_reviewed, status_code, indexability, word_count (diagnostic only), template, internal_inlinks, content_cluster, target_intent, conversion_or_task, traffic_window, source_of_record, observed_at.

## Criteria and pilot
Choose 5–8 purpose-specific qualitative criteria (book ch. 13 describes this as practical); do not enforce a universal Google requirement. Example: accuracy, unique contribution, task completion, audience fit, currency, accessibility, structure, policy risk. Define anchored PASS/NEEDS_WORK/FAIL/UNKNOWN rubrics, pilot 5–10 diverse pages as an internal implementation suggestion (not a book/Google threshold), reconcile disagreements before full audit.

## Content decisions
KEEP: valuable and current; UPDATE: useful but stale or unsupported; MERGE: overlapping pages where one stronger destination preserves distinct value; REWRITE: important page with weak structure/quality; REDIRECT: retired URL with genuinely equivalent destination; REMOVE: no ongoing purpose or value, after verifying traffic/backlinks/legal obligations; ESCALATE: legal/policy/YMYL uncertainty. Redirect is not a universal solution for unrelated pages.

## Evidence and measurement
Quantitative: impressions, clicks, page engagement, search intent, conversions, ad yield where available. Qualitative: accuracy, accessibility, usefulness, content gaps and journey coherence. Avoid ranking solely by traffic: low-traffic high-value service pages may matter.
Prioritization: impact × confidence × feasibility, with legal/policy blockers overriding score. This is an internal heuristic, not a source-derived formula.

## Delivery and governance
Produce an action register with page_id, diagnosis, evidence, proposed change, exact replacement copy or patch, owner, priority, deadline, dependencies, QA, rollback, next_review. Track changes through a repeatable editorial calendar and change log; distinguish proposals from actually applied CMS edits.
