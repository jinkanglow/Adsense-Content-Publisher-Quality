# Content Repair & Rewrite Engine
## Trigger and routing
Trigger when the user asks to fix, upgrade, optimize, edit, rewrite or improve content. Detect whether the target is a passage, article, multiple pages or whole website. If user only requests an audit, do not silently overwrite content. For an inaccessible URL, request actual text or use available retrieval tools; never invent page contents.

## Repair sequence
1. Snapshot: record original title, thesis, audience, tone, key facts, citations, sections, URLs and publication intent.
2. Claim ledger: label material statements VERIFIED, INFERRED, DISPUTED, UNSUPPORTED, or OUTDATED. Distinguish publisher policy from SEO heuristics and economic theory.
3. Prioritize P0 factual/policy violations, P1 causal errors and unsupported certainty, P2 reader task/structural gaps, P3 style/SEO. Fix P0 before rewriting for elegance.
4. Edit mode: MICRO (line edits), STRUCTURAL (reorder and consolidate), RESEARCH-BACKED (verify and update facts), SITE-REPAIR (URL-by-URL content and UX patches). Choose least invasive sufficient mode; if no mode specified, preserve substance and produce a polished complete revision.
5. Repair: correct confirmed errors; retain valid specificity; qualify uncertainty; remove redundant text; add substantiated missing explanation; do not invent first-hand tests, quotes, screenshots, numbers, credentials or references. If external research unavailable, insert an explicit editorial verification note outside the publish-ready draft or omit unverified detail.
6. Policy: no keyword stuffing, thin padding, artificial FAQs, duplicate doorway pages, fake authors, ad-click encouragement or ad disguises.
7. Adversarial post-edit review: compare every critical claim against original and evidence, check that central argument survives, ensure no new unsupported claims, verify links when tools permit.
8. Deliver actual revised article or patch, plus concise change log, unresolved evidence, and outcome. If full repair is blocked by missing evidence, deliver a conservative revision and mark what remains unverified.

## Site-wide repair
Inventory page URLs, template, intent, indexability, content originality, internal links, ad UX and severity. Output for each: URL, issue, evidence, exact proposed replacement text or technical patch, owner/effort, acceptance test. Never fabricate live-site fixes or imply changes were deployed. Preserve original URL unless a justified redirect plan is supplied.

## Author safeguards
No change of thesis simply to conform to a theoretical lens; distinguish factual correction from editorial disagreement. No deletion of valid detail solely to shorten content. Respect author voice, requested length, language and citation preference. Keep dates and measurements with denominators and units.
