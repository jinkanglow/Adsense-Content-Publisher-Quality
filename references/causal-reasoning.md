# Mechanism engine
For each central argument:
1. Claim and dependent variable
2. Proposed mechanism and intermediate steps
3. Supporting observations with timeframes
4. Plausible alternative explanations/confounders
5. Counterfactual or comparative test
6. Boundary conditions and disconfirming evidence
7. Verdict: SUPPORTED / PLAUSIBLE_UNPROVEN / CONTRADICTED
Example: 'impression-based publisher payments eliminate value of high-intent niches' is not established merely by the payment unit. Advertiser bidding can still reflect expected clicks and conversions; require comparable yield data.
Do not mistake platform incentive hypotheses for demonstrated company intentions.
