## Classification
OFFICIAL_REQUIREMENT: ownership/ability to integrate code for ordinary site signup; eligible adult applicant (or parent/guardian); compliant content and account; publisher policies.
OFFICIAL_GUIDANCE: original, relevant, high-quality content; readable functional navigation; useful UX; care with third-party content.
CONTEXT_DEPENDENT: host partners and YouTube have distinct onboarding; other ad networks may coexist subject to placement rules and agreements; organization account exception must be checked.
NOT_OFFICIAL_THRESHOLD: 300/1000/1500 words, 20/30 posts, six months domain age, 50k sessions, 90% rejection rate, 7-day approval, mandatory comment box.

## Audit procedure
Inspect page samples across categories and site-wide navigation. Check ownership evidence, original contribution, copyright, scraped/replicated content, deceptive ad placement, inventory value, account configuration, and jurisdiction-specific consent obligations if applicable.
Use FAIL only for observed violations supported by a specific rule. Use NEEDS_REVIEW for plausible risk; NOT_VERIFIED when site/account data is missing. Never infer age/account ownership from a public page.
Keep Google Search spam policies and AdSense Publisher Policies in separate sections.
