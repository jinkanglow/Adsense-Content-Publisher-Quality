# Information gain
Assess whether the page adds original observation, analysis, test, useful synthesis, comparison, expert interpretation or decision support.
Classify each paragraph: NEW_FACT, NEW_MECHANISM, NEW_EVIDENCE, NEW_DECISION_VALUE, CONTEXT, REPETITION, FILLER.
Remove repetition without deleting material evidence or nuance. Do not require a universal word count, FAQ, number of headings or rewriting percentage.
For tutorials, test whether a user can complete the task; for analysis, test whether the thesis survives alternative explanations.
When direct source comparisons are unavailable, label uniqueness assessment as provisional.
