# Editorial fact-checking — source-grounded procedure
Source: Brooke Borel, *The Chicago Guide to Fact-Checking*, 2nd ed. (2023), Introduction, chapters 2–6, especially chapter 3 (magazine/newspaper/hybrid models), chapter 4 (numbers, polls, quotes, concepts, images, headlines, conflicting facts, plagiarism), chapter 5 (sourcing), chapter 6 (records).

## Claim-level evidence ledger
For each material assertion record: claim_id, exact text, claim_type, materiality, source_title, source_url_or_file_locator, accessed_at, source_date, direct_support_excerpt_or_data_location, corroboration, calculation, status, repair_action. Valid statuses: VERIFIED, PARTIALLY_SUPPORTED, DISPUTED, FALSE, UNVERIFIED, NOT_CHECKABLE.
Separate attributed opinion, interpretation, inference, forecasts and empirically testable facts. Do not turn a source's assertion into verified truth merely by locating it. Check source authority, independence, primary record, date, context, units and denominator.

## Risk-tiered verification
Critical: accusations, legal claims, policy compliance, medical/financial safety, central numerical thesis, direct quotes, consequential headlines: independent check before publication. Important: other central facts: check original source and calculations. Low-risk: check names/dates/spellings with appropriate sources. Under time pressure use Borel's hybrid model; never silently skip high-risk checks.

## Specialized checks
- Statistics: period, geography, numerator/denominator, definition, base rate, sample, revisions, rounding and arithmetic.
- Quotes: verify original recording/transcript, exact wording, context, translation and attribution; otherwise paraphrase with disclosure.
- Images: provenance, permission, caption, location/time and whether illustrative rather than documentary.
- Policies: current official source and jurisdiction; books and SEO blogs cannot override policy.
- Disagreement: preserve competing source records and identify what would resolve it.
- Provenance: retain dated links, screenshots/notes where permitted, and a correction history; do not republish copyright-protected source passages.

## Independence
After rewrite, perform a second claim extraction without relying on the original ledger; compare and catch newly introduced claims. A single model pass is not independent verification: if no independent reviewer/tool is available label the second pass 'self-review', not 'independent fact-check'.
