# Publisher economics
Compute only from observed inputs:
Revenue = eligible pageviews / 1000 × observed page RPM (definition and denominator must be consistent).
Segment by geography, device, channel, page type and period where available.
Separate revenue change into traffic mix, inventory quantity, monetization yield, seasonality and policy/serving effects.
Track direct/return audience, email opt-in, referral diversity, revenue concentration, contribution margin and volatility.
Avoid universal traffic thresholds and promises that switching ad networks increases yield.
Do not treat correlation between a site edit and revenue change as causality; prefer controlled experiments where feasible.
