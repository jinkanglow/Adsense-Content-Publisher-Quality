# Platform dependency and chokepoint engine
Measure channel concentration (search, social, email, direct), revenue concentration by network, audience portability, contractual restrictions, data asymmetry and switching costs.
Simple scenario: lost-revenue exposure = current revenue × fraction attributable to affected channel × modeled traffic loss, holding yield constant. State the assumption; never imply a forecast.
Evaluate alternative monetization based on net revenue after fees, implementation costs, UX effects, termination and exclusivity. Thresholds are provider-specific and must be checked live.
Output: observed data, scenario assumptions, risk ranking, feasible mitigation, disconfirming evidence.
