# Value capture engine
Keep separate:
- Publisher content production cost
- Traffic acquisition and audience retention cost
- Monetizable pageviews and fill
- Gross and net publisher receipts
- Revenue per 1,000 pageviews using consistent denominator
- Platform/intermediary fees only when verified
- Operating profit and cash conversion
Do not assert advertiser spend = publisher revenue / 0.68 as a general rule; auction pathways and fees vary.
Measure reader trust and repeat traffic as strategic assets but do not invent their monetary valuation.
