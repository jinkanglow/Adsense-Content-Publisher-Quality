Page numbers below refer to PDF file page indexes displayed as 1-based PDF pages, not printed book pagination. These are book-author arguments, not independent empirical validation of current Google/AdSense behavior. No direct quotations required.

## A. Tim Wu — The Attention Merchants (PDF TEXT VERIFIED)
Source locations: Introduction pp. 10–13; Ch. 1 'The First Attention Merchants' pp. 18–22; Ch. 20 'The Kingdom of Content' pp. 295–298; Ch. 22 'The Rise of Clickbait' pp. 319 onward; Ch. 26 'The Web Hits Bottom' pp. 366–376; Ch. 27 'A Retreat and a Revolt' pp. 377–386; Epilogue pp. 392–395.

Mechanism: an apparently free content/service bargain is financed by reselling audience attention to advertisers. The publisher's incentive to maximize salable attention can diverge from the reader's interest in high-quality, uninterrupted information. Wu also describes reactions against excessive commercialization, and demand for deeper, sustained attention.
Engineering translation:
- ATTENTION_BARGAIN: identify the value received by reader relative to interruptions, misleading headlines and attention cost.
- HEADLINE_DELIVERY_GAP: test whether headline promise matches what the page actually delivers.
- INTERRUPTION_BUDGET: audit ad placements, intrusive interstitials and content obscuration; report measured interference, not guessed attention span.
- TRUST_RETENTION: compare return visits, direct traffic and task success if available, rather than optimizing raw time-on-page alone.
Boundary: high engagement is not inherently manipulation; a short visit may indicate successful task completion. The book does not prescribe Google AdSense ad-density thresholds.

## B. Nick Srnicek — Platform Capitalism (PDF TEXT VERIFIED)
Source locations: Introduction pp. 12–15; Ch. 2 platform model pp. 39–43; 'Advertising Platforms' pp. 44–48; Ch. 3 'Great Platform Wars' pp. 75–85, especially 'Tendencies' pp. 78–84.

Mechanism: platforms mediate interactions, capture and analyze data, benefit from network effects and can set participation rules; competition encourages data extraction, gatekeeping, convergence and ecosystem enclosure. His five platform types are advertising, cloud, industrial, product and lean platforms.
Engineering translation:
- PLATFORM_DEPENDENCY: measure search/referral shares, concentration and revenue sensitivity to changes in a single intermediary.
- DATA_ASYMMETRY: document what publisher can observe versus what cannot be verified about auctions and user behavior.
- GATEKEEPER_RULES: monitor actual contractual/policy requirements, without asserting motivations as facts.
- SWITCHING_COST: inventory costs and viable alternatives for audience acquisition and monetization.
Boundary: platform dynamics are a theory, not proof of Google exercising particular pricing discretion or intent in a given period; 2017 descriptions are historical, not current policy.

## C. Rebecca Giblin & Cory Doctorow — Chokepoint Capitalism (EPUB INSPECTED)
Source locations: chapters 'How News Got Broken', 'How Amazon Took Over Books', 'Why Spotify Wants You to Rely on Playlists'.
Mechanism: chokepoints between creators and their audiences can limit alternatives and weaken bargaining leverage; gatekeeper control and contractual terms matter.
Engineering translation:
- CHOKEPOINT_MAP: record who controls discovery, distribution, monetization, identity and customer relationship.
- EXIT_OPTION: compare alternative channels and assess audience portability.
- CONTRACT_AUDIT: exclusivity, termination, rev-share and data access only when actual terms are supplied.
Boundary: examples from music/news/books do not automatically establish AdSense-specific terms or effects.

## D. Tim Wu — The Age of Extraction (EPUB INSPECTED)
Source locations: parts 'Understanding Platform Power' and 'The Dangers of Centralized Economic Power'.
Mechanism: distinguish total value produced within an ecosystem from the portion appropriated or retained by its intermediaries.
Engineering translation:
- VALUE_CAPTURE_LEDGER: separate advertiser spend, intermediary fees (only if evidenced), publisher receipts, operating costs and retained margin.
- ECONOMIC_RESILIENCE: measure diversification and dependency-adjusted risk.
Boundary: do not infer a numerical 'extraction rate' without actual payment and contract data.

## Cross-book synthesis
Reader attention -> content/service exchange -> platform-mediated distribution -> gatekeeper/auction -> publisher revenue -> retained economic value.
These are different analytical layers, not a single established causal chain for any particular site. Verify each empirical link.

## Audit decision rules
1. Official Google policy controls compliance verdicts, never book theories.
2. Content quality requires factual accuracy and task completion, not maximal attention.
3. Assess intermediary dependence only when channel data is available.
4. Flag unsupported claims of deliberate suppression, exploitation or price manipulation.
5. Do not force all four lenses into routine how-to pages.
