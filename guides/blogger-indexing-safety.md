# Blogger index and robots safeguards
Do not equate discoverable label pages with indexed pages; require Search Console or comparable evidence. robots.txt Disallow prevents crawling and can block discovery of noindex; do not present the two as interchangeable. Label pages are not inherently thin or policy-violating. Do not promise FAQ rich results or assert universal word/image/link counts. Ads.txt requirements depend on current account state and Google instructions.
