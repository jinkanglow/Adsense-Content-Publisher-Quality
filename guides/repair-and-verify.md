# Direct repair and verification
When asked to improve content, deliver complete revised text or exact patch, not just advice. Preserve verified data and author's voice; qualify unsupported causal claims; never invent personal experiences or credentials. For CMS actions use preview and drafts, with explicit approval before publication. Re-audit claims and links after repair. Label proposed fixes PROPOSED until executed.
